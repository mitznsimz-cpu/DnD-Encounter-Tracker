Help & About

MitzNSimz Encounter Tracker · v1.3

Keyboard Shortcuts

NAdvance to next turn
EscClose open panels and pickers
EnterApply damage (when damage field focused)
HApply as healing (when damage field focused)
Ctrl+ZUndo last action (coming soon)

Storage & Data

EncounterSaved automatically to localStorage on every change. Close and reopen — it resumes exactly where you left off.
Monster DB~3,200 monsters and ~1,400 spells cached in IndexedDB (no size limit). Downloaded once on first load.
Custom dataCustom monsters and spells stored in localStorage alongside your encounter library.
Clearing dataUse New to clear the encounter. Use Refresh DB (in header) to re-download the monster database.

Offline Use

After first loadFully offline. The monster database is cached and all functionality works without internet.
First loadRequires internet to download the monster database (~3–5 MB). Open once with internet before your session.
FontsCinzel and Crimson Pro load from Google Fonts. If offline, the app falls back to Palatino/Georgia — still readable.

Known Limitations

RulesetD&D 5e 2014. The 2024 / 5.5e ruleset is planned for a future update.
Monster DBContains SRD and OGL sourcebook monsters. Adventure-specific monsters (e.g. from published adventures) may not be included — use the custom monster editor or stat block parser.
No gridThe tracker has no map or grid view. It is designed to work alongside physical tiles and miniatures.
Single userSingle-browser only. No multi-DM or player-facing shared view.
